Admin Portal – All‑rounder Tool for Staffing Companies

  • End‑to‑end hiring stack: job posting, candidate sourcing, interview scheduling.
  • JD‑to‑Candidate matching pulling active candidates from job ports.
  • AI‑driven first‑level interview: question bank from JD, AI calls candidate, evaluates ~90% answer match.
  • Second‑level human interview feedback captured instantly.
  • One‑click CV move to HR, automatic BGV trigger, health‑check & drug‑test status.
  • Bulk document release to BGV team; auto‑generated offer letters.
  • Rejection analytics with AI suggestions for next best jobs.
  • Real‑time status dashboard pinpointing where a pipeline gets stuck.
  • Searchable candidate repository: CV, education, ID/proof documents.

Candidate Portal – Your Career Dashboard

  • Job discovery with keyword, skill, experience, location, and date filters.
  • Upload CV, personal data, education & experience documents once; auto‑populate applications.
  • Application tracker visual pipeline: Profile submitted → First round clear → Second round clear → Manager clear → BGV clear → On‑boarding → Offer.
  • Positive feedback after each round (e.g., “Strong technical skills; consider improving communication”) plus institute recommendations for upskilling.
  • AI‑driven job recommendations based on profile & feedback.
  • One‑click download of offer letter.

Client Portal – Control Tower

  • Upload JD with skills, experience, rate card, notice period, BGV requirements.
  • View shortlisted CVs with AI match scores.
  • Schedule interviews & auto‑generate gate‑passes for candidates.
  • Immediate feedback on status board visible to candidate & admin.
  • Rate & notice‑period validation against candidate profile.
  • Track BGV progress, health‑check, document clearance.
  • One‑click document dispatch to BGV team.
